# Experiment and data contract — v1.1

**Question.** For a currently active fictional mobile-money customer, can the preceding 60 days predict no successful transactions during the next 30 days? Compare model quality and integration effort, without treating synthetic findings as evidence of real-world performance.

**Unit and time.** One unique `(customer_id, prediction_date)` snapshot. All dates are fictional UTC days. At midnight `t`, features use `[t−60 days, t)`; active means at least one success in `[t−30 days, t)`; `dormant_30d=1` means zero successes in `[t, t+30 days)`. Require complete 60-day history and 30-day future coverage; exclude censored snapshots. Events at `t` belong only to outcomes. Final statuses are immediate in this simulation; a reversed attempt never counts as a success.

**Source schema.** Generated only: `customers.csv` has `customer_id:str` (unique `SYN-C######`), `archetype:str`, `region:str`, `channel:str`, `is_synthetic:bool`. `events.csv` has unique `event_id:str`, customer FK, `event_date:date`, `product:str`, `amount_minor:int>0` in fictional units, `status:success|failed|reversed`, `is_synthetic:bool`. Region/channel are fixed fictional attributes; products are fictional transfer, merchant and cash services. No nulls in source tables. Manifest records schema/version, generator/version/source hashes, seeds, coverage, counts and file hashes. Every derived row retains `is_synthetic=true`.

**Feature contract.** Use successes unless specified. Recency = days since latest success; frequency and value trends = `(recent30−prior30)/(prior30+1)`; gap trend = recent mean consecutive-success day gap minus prior mean gap (null if either half has fewer than two successes); diversity = distinct products in 60 days; merchant adoption = any merchant success in 60 days; concentration = largest product share of successful value in 60 days; active-day trend uses the same smoothed formula as frequency; failure/reversal rate = `(failed+reversed)/all attempts` in 60 days. Include fixed channel and region. Report numeric units and missingness. Exclude IDs, dates, labels, future counts, split assignment, synthetic flag and latent archetype from predictors. Activity slices use successful 60-day counts: low 1–5, medium 6–20, high 21+; archetype is evaluation metadata only.

**Evaluation design.** Coverage `[2032-01-01, 2032-11-01)`. SHA-256 of `split_seed:customer_id`, modulo 100, assigns customer groups: 0–59 train, 60–79 validation, 80–99 test. Seed 3501. Train snapshots: 1 Apr, 1 May, 1 Jun; validation: 1 Aug; test: 1 Oct, all 2032. Use only each group's dates; labels mature before the next evaluation origin. Fit transforms on train, choose settings/calibration/thresholds on validation, then freeze before test. This evaluates later unseen customers. Random reference: pool eligible snapshots across these five dates and all groups, stratified row split 60/20/20, seed 3501, same features/models and validation procedure. Explicitly report customer overlap, sizes and prevalence; its difference combines temporal shift and group leakage and is not a causal estimate. Random splits are not deployment-valid.

**Models and scoring.** Constant-prior check; logistic regression; XGBoost primary tree comparator; CatBoost challenger; TabPFN-3.5 base, Fast and Thinking subject to access. Label hosted Plus separately. Primary PR-AUC is average precision (not trapezoidal integration); also log loss, Brier, 10 fixed-bin reliability plot/ECE, and top 5%/10% capture and lift. Select `k=ceil(q*n)` by descending probability with snapshot-key ties: capture=`TP@k/all positives`, lift=`(TP@k/k)/prevalence`. Report FP/FN at 0.5 and each top-k budget. Calibration, if added, uses validation only and raw/calibrated results stay separate. Show per-archetype/activity support, positives, metrics and customer-bootstrap intervals; mark empty/single-class ranking metrics unavailable. Report signed random-minus-temporal differences for each metric and its direction; negative optimism gaps are allowed.

**Execution and stop rule.** Fixed benchmark: 15,000 customers, approved by the user before model fitting to strengthen outcome and subgroup support. Preserve the earlier 600/1,800-customer pilots (`M1_PILOT_RESULTS.md`); this decision supersedes their population cap. Minimum feasibility support remains 50 rows and 10 of each label per main partition, with sparse slices disclosed (`M1_SPEC.md`). Primary seed 3501; sensitivity seeds 3502/3503; split seed fixed at 3501. Log fit/preprocessing/predict and end-to-end latency, hardware, cold/warm status, repetitions, model/version/settings, data hashes, estimated/actual tokens and skip reasons. API estimates must use current metering; token counts are not currency prices. No uploads or resource creation without explicit approval. Success is a reproducible, honest report and integration record, not a required model ranking.
