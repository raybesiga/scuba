4. Judging
4.1 Entries are reviewed and winners are selected by a panel of judges appointed by Prior Labs from among its personnel.

4.2 The objective of the Hackathon is to build a working project that demonstrably showcases the capabilities of TabPFN-3.5. Entries are evaluated by the judges against the following criteria: (i) Showcase of TabPFN-3.5 (50%) - how prominently and effectively the project uses TabPFN-3.5 and how convincingly it demonstrates the model's capabilities in a working prototype; (ii) Creativity and originality (30%) - how novel and inventive the idea is and what practical value it delivers; and (iii) Technical quality and reproducibility (20%) - the quality of the implementation and whether the project can be reproduced from the linked repository in accordance with clause 4.2.

4.3 The judges select the top 3 participants (the "Winners") and may additionally award honorable mentions.

4.4 The judges' decisions, including which entries are in scope, are final. Within the criteria and weightings set out in clause 5.2, the judges have reasonable discretion in assessing the entries. Recourse to the courts in respect of the selection of winners is excluded.

4.5 Winners are notified using the contact details associated with their account. A winner must respond within seven (7) days of notification; if a winner does not respond in time, the entitlement to recognition or to any prize may lapse and may be awarded to the next-ranked entry.
